# Salon Booking – Android Application

A multi-salon beauty and wellness booking application that connects customers with beauty salons in their neighborhoods.

The application allows customers to discover nearby salons, explore beauty services, select their preferred staff members, and book appointments in advance.

The main goal is to eliminate long waiting times by allowing customers to reserve their preferred service, staff member, and appointment time before visiting the salon.

## 🎯 About the Project

Salon Booking is an Android application designed to make salon reservations easier, faster, and more convenient.

The platform supports multiple beauty salons, allowing each salon to manage its services, employees, schedules, and customer appointments.

Customers can choose from different beauty services, select a specific staff member, and reserve an available appointment.

## ✨ Main Features

### 👤 User Authentication & Profile
- User registration and login.
- Firebase Authentication.
- Personal customer profiles.
- Profile photo and personal information.
- Update account details.
- View appointment history.
- Manage upcoming bookings.

### 📍 Nearby Beauty Salons
- Discover beauty salons in nearby neighborhoods.
- Browse multiple registered salons.
- View salon profiles and information.
- Search salons by name or location.
- View salon addresses and contact information.
- View salon photos and working hours.
- Check available services.
- View customer ratings and reviews.

### 💅 Beauty Services

Customers can explore different beauty services, including:

- **Henna:** Different henna designs and styles.
- **Hair:** Haircuts, hairstyling, washing, and treatments.
- **Nails:** Manicure, pedicure, and nail art.
- **Spa:** Relaxation and beauty treatments.
- **Makeup:** Bridal and special-event makeup.
- **Skincare:** Facial treatments and skincare services.
- **Other Services:** Additional services offered by each salon.

### 👩‍💼 Staff Selection

Customers can choose their preferred beauty professional instead of being assigned someone randomly.

- View available salon employees.
- See staff names and profile photos.
- Explore each employee's skills and specialties.
- View staff ratings and reviews.
- Select a preferred henna artist, hairstylist, nail technician, or other professional.
- Check employee availability.
- Book a specific staff member.

### 📅 Appointment Booking

The appointment booking system helps customers avoid waiting at salons.

- Select a beauty salon.
- Choose the required service.
- Select a preferred staff member.
- Choose an available date.
- Select an available appointment time.
- View service prices and duration.
- Confirm the appointment.
- Receive booking confirmation.
- Prevent double bookings for the same staff member and time slot.

### ⏰ No-Waiting Appointment System

The main feature of the application is appointment scheduling.

Customers reserve a specific time before visiting the salon. When they arrive at their scheduled time, the selected employee is ready to provide the booked service.

The system helps:
- Reduce customer waiting times.
- Organize salon schedules.
- Prevent appointment conflicts.
- Help employees manage their daily appointments.
- Improve the customer experience.

### 📋 My Bookings

Customers can:
- View upcoming appointments.
- View completed appointments.
- Check booking dates and times.
- See selected salons and staff members.
- View service details and prices.
- Cancel or reschedule eligible appointments.
- Check appointment status.

### ⭐ Ratings & Reviews
- Rate beauty salons.
- Review salon services.
- Rate individual staff members.
- Share customer experiences.
- Display average ratings.
- Help customers choose trusted salons and professionals.

## 🔄 How It Works

### Customer Workflow

1. Register or log in.
2. Discover nearby beauty salons.
3. Select a preferred salon.
4. Browse available services.
5. Choose a specific service.
6. Select a preferred employee.
7. Choose an available date and time.
8. Confirm the appointment.
9. Visit the salon at the reserved time.
10. Receive the service without unnecessary waiting.
11. Rate and review the experience.

### Salon Owner Workflow

1. Register the salon.
2. Wait for platform admin approval, if required.
3. Create the salon profile.
4. Add beauty services and prices.
5. Add employees and their specialties.
6. Set working hours and employee availability.
7. Receive customer bookings.
8. Manage appointments and schedules.
9. Provide services to customers.

### Platform Admin Workflow

1. Log in to the admin dashboard.
2. Manage registered users.
3. Review salon registration requests.
4. Approve or reject salons.
5. Monitor salon activities.
6. Manage platform categories and services.
7. Monitor bookings and platform statistics.
